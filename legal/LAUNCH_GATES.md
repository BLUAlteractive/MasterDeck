# Launch compliance checklist (for professional review)

These are product risk controls, not a legal determination.

- [ ] Original mark/name and trademark search; no unlicensed artwork, card back or vendor association.
- [ ] Contracts assign rights to artwork, music and code and document commercial AI-art provenance.
- [ ] Counsel checks social poker, virtual chips, tournaments and geo-specific gaming restrictions.
- [ ] No real-money-value wagers, crypto/NFT wagers, cash-out, stakeable paid currency or gift card prize pools.
- [ ] Store age category / availability matches operating jurisdictions.
- [ ] Apple/Google store billing and account deletion guidance checked against live published policies.
- [ ] Digital goods purchased via allowed store mechanism, signed server verified, refunded consistently.
- [ ] If any paid random packs later enabled: odds disclosure, item policies and consumer protection reviewed.
- [ ] If any NFT later enabled: store policy, consumer law, securities/AML/KYC/tax, audits and geography approved.
- [ ] Health/activity and separately opted-in foreground geo consent, safe public-route approval, accessible alternatives, short evidence TTL, data minimization and deletion tested.
- [ ] Privacy policy, terms, support escalation, moderation/reporting and complaints channel published.
- [ ] Independent security review on game state, fraud, spending and transaction flows.
- [ ] Tournament rules show eligibility, tie resolution, cancellations and noncash rewards.
- [ ] No real brand/physical prize campaign before campaign-specific agreement and counsel sign-off.

Primary sources in `MASTER_SPEC.md` §23; recheck shortly before release.

### Additional v1.1 release gates
- [ ] Reward routes and drop locations legally accessible in public areas and reviewed for dangers, third-party brand misuse, prohibited sensitive places and geodata license/attribution.
- [ ] Press Screen paid art cannot change step quotas, upgrade power, active jobs or earned reward rates; functional screens paid ONLY in earned Trail Credits.
- [ ] All IAP card packs have disclosed **fixed cosmetic contents**, verified receipts and refund policy; paid randomized packs are disabled.
- [ ] Earned random pack pool tables and published odds are versioned; server-side sampled, fully ledgered and guarded against abuse.
- [ ] iOS/Android real-device test verifies foreground location consent and HealthKit/Health Connect permissions separately, including revoke and denial.
- [ ] Emergency operational feature flags disable unsafe routes, bad drops or mispriced packs, without corrupting cards already issued.
