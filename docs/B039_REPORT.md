# B039 — Fixed cosmetic storefront

Added versioned fixed-content cosmetic catalog products, original presentation assets, private distinct-item entitlement pagination and a mobile storefront preview. Presentation accepts only item kind, colors and a closed motif set; level, rarity, earned style, fusion, press, route or scoring modifiers are rejected. Colors/patterns are original code-native assets, with readable labels outside the swatches. No third-party artwork, random paid contents, payment command, purchase grant or currency conversion is introduced.

Products start in DRAFT; publish requires explicit product/item approval and 1–20 exact known contents. Published terms/contents cannot change; retirement preserves evidence. An operator-reviewed allow-list controls catalog visibility and defaults absent in main. Prices include explicit currency/minor-unit scale and are labeled references; the native platform must supply its localized final quote before any purchase. Checkout remains unavailable in this build. Entitlements are owner-scoped and queried as distinct items before pagination, so duplicate purchase grants cannot crowd other items out of the view.

Before commerce activation, review the enumerated assumptions in [COMMERCE_REVIEW.md](COMMERCE_REVIEW.md): fixed nonconsumable original cosmetics, permanent-account binding, platform verification, rights/product/price and age/region review, protected credentials, and refund/restore privacy. This document and test fixtures are not product/legal approval. No live product or price is selected or published to a store.
