# Moderation and incident boundary

`GET /v1/safety/rooms/:game/:id` is participant-only and returns event-local seats plus the caller's block/report state. `POST /v1/safety/commands` accepts strict participant reports or explicit SET_BLOCK commands. Immutable reports bind to the observed server state version/hash; retries retain their receipt. Blocks have append-only versioned evidence and apply in both directions to future public/private/tournament admissions. The table safety panel exposes these actions without free-text chat, public account search or raw evidence upload.

Blocking does not rewrite a current game, forfeit a player, cancel escrow or award a win. Existing game authorization remains in force. Suspension revokes sessions and excludes the account from new authorized requests/admissions. Open non-tournament queues are cleared safely; active game/tournament locks and earned-chip settlement remain governed by their original server state machines. Spades/abandoned-tournament incidents can require operator resolution; do not improvise a result or unlock inventory without a reviewed recovery procedure.

`POST /v1/safety/admin` requires a current SUPPORT capability, permanent ADMIN identity and MFA. Actions include SUSPEND/REINSTATE, INSPECT and REVIEW_REPORT. Every action/read inspection requires a reason, scope, idempotency UUID and immutable AdminAction. Reinstatement does not revive old sessions and cannot bypass a deletion request. Self-suspension is refused. There is no public scope provisioning, arbitrary result override or balance editor.

Inspection recomputes poker/Spades state from immutable genesis and commands, verifies snapshots and returns version/state/history hashes plus the latest 100 event proofs. Raw shuffle order, hidden cards, private snapshots and credentials stay server-private. A report review decision records staff triage, not a legal conclusion or automatic refund. Tournament result reports remain in B042's distinct immutable record and audited replay path.

Monitor failed authorization/replay, case backlog, suspension/session consistency and active rooms awaiting recovery. Log fixed event/status names rather than participant IDs, report contents or tokens. Product/support must review escalation, appeals, abusive-report rates, staffing and retention before beta. Publication of terms/moderation policy is a launch gate, not established by these tests. Owned rollback removes disposable case/block fixtures only; production repair preserves evidence and uses audited forward changes.
