B053 prepares reviewable launch controls; it does not deploy, sign a checklist on someone's behalf or certify a production rollback. `ops/release-manifest.json` contains no artifact, resources, evidence or approvals. A successful build or a signed TEST ONLY fixture is never a real release approval.

## Signed checklist protocol

Pin an actual 40-character artifact commit, exact HTTPS API/admin/CORS origins and nonsecret resource/key-set references. References describe separately protected deployment resources, never database passwords or API keys. Attach versioned HTTPS evidence references and SHA-256 digests after reviewers actually inspect their contents. Query-bearing or credential-bearing evidence URLs are forbidden. No crawler verifies remote evidence: the reviewer attests its identity, digest and meaning. Evidence access/retention and reviewer authority require the owner's protected process.

The reviewed registry contains only Ed25519 **public** keys, approved roles and revocation state. Product, Security, Platform, Privacy, Legal and Operations provision their own signing access outside this repository. Protect registry/manifest changes with actual owner-controlled branch review; this repository has not configured organizational reviewers, branch protection or a signing service. A newly generated key does not establish legal or organizational authority. At least two distinct approved keys are required; assigning roles does not itself establish independent people.

Populate all evidence records before signing. `releaseDigest` hashes the canonical artifact, target, origins, resource references, flags, gate statuses and evidence digests/references, excluding signature metadata. `releaseApprovalPayload` binds that digest, gate, required role, key ID, reviewer label and signed timestamp to the `deckforge-release-approval-v1` domain. Sign its exact bytes with the separately held private key. Each signature must match a nonrevoked trusted role and be no more than 30 days old or five minutes in the future. Any artifact/flag/evidence change invalidates approvals and requires review again. The CLI compares the manifest artifact with actual Git HEAD. Signature validity proves possession of a registered key and unchanged attestation, not correctness of a legal/provider/device claim.

This is a preflight gate and evidence protocol, not a deployed feature-switch service or permission to send traffic. Do not automate deployment from `release:validate`; only `release:check` can be a required approval step, and an operator still confirms independent evidence and target resources. Keep the real manifest attached to its release artifact outside speculative source edits; the committed manifest remains an unsigned template until reviewed.

## Promotion and rollback

1. Build immutable artifacts from the pinned clean commit; run frozen install, quality, security/dependency audit and owned recovery checks. Preserve build/schema/asset digests and toolchain/device evidence.
2. In a separately authorized isolated staging environment, disable new admissions/sales/rewards, restore a compatible snapshot/assets and protected keys, and verify privacy tombstones and ledger reconciliation. Do not import unminimized production data into a development workstation.
3. Exercise auth/refresh/revoke, two-device private card projection, earned-only balances, inventory/fusion idempotency, platform purchase/restore/refund and safe activity/route journeys. Deliver a synthetic aggregate alert to the approved destination and confirm operator acknowledgement. Record actual timing and outcomes; do not mark a document as an executed drill.
4. Rehearse application rollback to the previous compatible immutable artifact with durable schema/history preserved. Open an owned staged hand before the change; lose an acknowledgement, restart the actor, privately resume and retry the same command; verify one settlement and unchanged balances/card instances. Confirm pending receipts/deletions survive. Record target/artifact IDs, evidence digest, observer and actual result.
5. Review signed gates for the exact target and artifact; independently compare protected resources, flags and approvals. Only the human-authorized deployment process may promote or cut traffic. No production promotion was executed by this ticket.
6. If health/integrity fails, stop new work and sales, preserve accepted work and restrict affected traffic. Route to a previously validated compatible artifact; keep durable journals/event/ownership/purchase/deletion evidence. Never run `prisma/rollback` or `migrate reset` against production. Forward repair incompatible migrations under a reviewed plan; do not edit balances/events or manufacture replacement grants.
